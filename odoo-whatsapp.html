The context

Monotaro Indonesia connects business buyers with many suppliers. For products without known supplier stock, procurement needs to check availability before proceeding. As buyer demand increased, a small procurement team had more supplier follow-ups to handle. RFQs already went out by email, but many suppliers were more responsive on WhatsApp. The project brings those procurement actions into the channel suppliers actually use.

The procurement workflow

What the integration automates.

  1. 01

    RFQ inquiry & reply review

    A procurement team member sends an RFQ inquiry with a button in Odoo. A batch process analyzes supplier replies using AI and updates the purchase order’s RFQ WA Status field, including statuses for problems such as a price mismatch. Clean results can proceed to automatic RFQ confirmation and PO sending.

    Incoming messages are collected by a GCP instance, stored in Cloud SQL, and flow into BigQuery. Functions and a task queue also deliver messages to the Chatwoot conversation interface.

  2. 02

    Purchase-order delivery

    Confirming an RFQ triggers delivery of the PO document to the supplier through WhatsApp.

  3. 03

    Payment-document delivery

    Procurement emails are ingested into BigQuery, and bank payment notifications are converted into PDFs. The document is passed to Odoo for delivery through WhatsApp, reducing manual payment notifications.

  4. 04

    Pickup scheduling

    Procurement sets the pickup field in Odoo, which triggers the supplier message with the pickup schedule.

  5. 05

    Alternative supplier outreach

    When the primary supplier has no stock, the vendor-alternative team can trigger outreach to up to five alternative suppliers per SKU with a button, replacing separate manual messages to each vendor.

Design decisions

Designing around supplier behavior.

Meet suppliers in their existing channel

Email RFQs were already part of the process, but many went unanswered. WhatsApp group conversations fit how these suppliers actually communicated, so the integration was shaped around that workflow.

Make the message easier to answer

The RFQ templates were refined with clearer wording and an easier reply format, helping suppliers respond to the inquiry in a useful way.
